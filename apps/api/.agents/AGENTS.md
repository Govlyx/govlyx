# Workspace Agent Rules

## Automatic Graphify Updates
Whenever you complete a task that involves making structural code changes (such as creating a new class, modifying dependencies, or significantly changing a service), you MUST automatically update the project's graphify memory. You can do this by regenerating the architecture map and saving it to `.agents/references/architecture_map.md` in the current project.

## Marketing & Product Positioning Guidelines
Whenever asked for marketing strategies, growth ideas, or branding for this project (Govlyx / Jan-Sahayak-AI):
- **NEVER** treat or pitch the app solely or primarily as a civic complaint / issue reporting tool. Issue reporting is just one utility layer.
- **ALWAYS** position it as a vibrant **Hyper-Local Neighborhood Social & Discovery Super-App** ("Your Pincode's Living Room" / "Nextdoor + Reddit + Anonymous Nearby Chat"), and provide ideas across all of the following core pillars:
  1. **Neighborhood Wisdom & "Ask Locally"**: Word-of-mouth Q&A for trusted local recommendations (best food spots, broadband reliability, reliable domestic help, doctors, flats) from real neighbors.
  2. **QuickChat & Anonymous Nearby Banter**: Ephemeral 1v1 anonymous chatting with nearby people (within 3–5 km radius) for college students, flatmates, and young residents without sharing phone numbers or personal handles.
  3. **Neighborhood Pulse & Dynamic Polls**: High-engagement local debates and polls (society issues, local market changes, power/water outages, fun neighborhood sentiments).
  4. **Micro-Communities & Local Meetups**: Pincode-level interest circles (early morning runners, pet parents, badminton/box cricket, board game nights) to meet neighbors in walking distance.
  5. **Urgent Neighborhood Help & Lost & Found**: High-trust utility for lost pets, keys, local emergencies, and nearby blood donor requests.
  6. **Civic Accountability & Official Broadcasts (Supporting Utility)**: Pincode-scoped official department updates and SLA-tracked resolution cards.
