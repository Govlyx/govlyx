# Govlyx-AI Architecture Map

## Overview

Govlyx-AI is a Spring Boot application acting as a platform for citizen grievances, government civic broadcasting, and community interaction. It features citizen-to-citizen anonymous chatting, community management, post creation/resolution, HLIG hyper-local feed ranking, real-time messaging, and SLA monitoring.

## Core Domain Models

- `User`: Platform users with roles (`ROLE_USER`, `ROLE_DEPARTMENT`, `ROLE_ADMIN`). Supports OAuth2 (`googleId`, `authProvider`), geocoding/pincodes, preferences (e.g. `theme` with light/dark persistence).
  - **Persona Mirroring**: Holds full mirror of public persona and preference attributes (`username` pseudonym, `profileImage`, `bio`, `pincode`, `homeLatitude`, `homeLongitude`, `mutedWords`, `blockedActors`, `profanityFilterLevel`, `copyrightStrikes`, `isAdult`, `theme`, `interfaceLanguage`, `preferredLanguage`, `autoTranslate`). Serves as the single permanent source of truth for user persona settings across author token rotations.
- `Post`: Civic issue reports and official government/department broadcasts. Supports multi-level geographic targeting (`AREA`, `DISTRICT`, `STATE`, `COUNTRY`), resolution tracking, and denormalized engagement counters (`likeCount`, `dislikeCount`, `commentCount`, `shareCount`, `viewCount`, `saveCount`).
- `SocialPost`: Public social discussions and localized citizen posts. Includes category, hashtag indexing, virality/engagement scoring, and embedded polling.
- `Community`: Geographic or topical communities with member roles, privacy levels, and dynamic health scores.
- `CommunityMember`: Membership mappings with composite roles.
- `Poll`, `PollOption`, `PollVote`: Dynamic polling attached to social posts with flexible durations (1H, 1D, 3D, 7D, Always/indefinite), multiple-vote configuration, and vote percentage analytics.
- `PostShare`: Denormalized and breakdown-tracked sharing records for posts and social posts with share types (`LINK_COPY`, `EXTERNAL_SHARE`).
- `Comment` & `CommentInteraction`: Hierarchical multi-tier comments with parent-reply threading, upvotes/downvotes, and atomic cascade counter decrements.
- `ChatSession` & `ChatSessionAudit`: Ephemeral 1-vs-1 chat session tracking and persistent audit logging. Audit logging is selectively bypassed for test/synthetic accounts matching configured regex patterns (e.g., `^(user\d+|usersomenumer)@gmail\.com$`).
- `PostTranslation`: Cached multi-lingual translations for posts and social content.
- `Notification`: In-app notification delivery across civic broadcasts, community invitations, join requests, comment replies, post interactions, and role updates. Supports unread tracking, cursor pagination, and real-time delivery via WebSocket/WebPush.
- `NotificationSummaryDto`: Compact projection DTO used for instant single-roundtrip tab count calculations.

- `ActorProfile`: Approach A civic persona registry. Decoupled public social/civic identity for citizens identified by a pseudonymous `actor_token` (`act_...`), holding system `username` pseudonym, avatar, bio, pincode, home coordinates, and moderation settings. `display_name` has been completely eliminated across database and models; the auto-generated unique `username` pseudonym is the universal author handle everywhere.
- `BannedActor`: Cryptographic scammer and content-abuse registry banning bad actors by `actor_token` without deanonymizing their authentication vault.
- `AdminAuditLog`: Statutory CERT-In non-repudiation audit trail recording administrative enforcement actions.

## Key Services & Controllers

- `IdentityBlindService`: Core cryptographic engine computing deterministic server actor tokens (`server_actor_token = HMAC(userId + actorSalt, blindPepper)`), blind index hashes (`email_hash = HMAC(email, emailPepper)`), and client actor tokens (`act_ + HMAC(serverActorToken, seedBlindSalt)`).
- `AesGcmEmailConverter`: JPA AttributeConverter encrypting/decrypting sensitive citizen emails at rest using AES-256-GCM with dynamic 12-byte random IVs.
- `CivicPseudonymService`: Dedicated cryptographic pseudonym engine minting unique, non-colliding civic handles (e.g. `WildDragon5670`, `SharpDolphin4874`) from curated adjective + noun + number pools, verified for absolute uniqueness across both `actor_profiles` and `users` tables.
- `ActorProfileService` & `ActorProfileController`:
  - Public persona resolution, profile updates, and pseudonym management.
  - **Model 1 Clean Break Zero-Knowledge Persona Engine (`createOrCopyFromUser`)**:
    - When a new author token arrives (on first login or identity reset), mints a fresh, independent civic pseudonym specifically for that `actor_token` via `CivicPseudonymService` or registers a sanitized requested custom pseudonym.
    - Enforces complete forensic decoupling: citizen accounts in `users` table store strictly opaque internal account handles (`acc_<uuid>`) and NEVER store public pseudonyms.
    - Zero cross-table correlation: `SELECT * FROM users u JOIN actor_profiles a ON u.username = a.username;` returns 0 rows.
    - When a citizen rotates their author token or resets their PIN on a new device, their new author token gets a brand-new pseudonym, while historical posts remain permanently anonymous under their previous pseudonym (no deanonymizing migration).
- `ActorBanService`: Moderator and administrative service for banning offending `actor_token` personas across feed and interactions.
- `ActorTokenBackfillRunner`: Idempotent chunked migration runner executing zero-data-loss backfills of `actor_token`, author snapshots, and email encryption.
- `NotificationController` & `NotificationService`: Real-time notification aggregation and cursor-paginated delivery with server-side category tab filtering (`all`, `unread`, `invites`, `interactions`), single-roundtrip aggregate summary count projections (`GET /api/notifications/counts`), batch join-request prefetching to eliminate N+1 queries, and bulk database state updates (`markAllAsReadForUser`).
- `FeedController` & `HLIGFeedService`: High-performance multi-tier feed recommendation system (FOR_YOU, LOCATION, FOLLOWING, OFFICIAL, NEIGHBORHOOD_QA) with Caffeine candidate caching (`hlig_feed`), phase-based scoring, diversity injection, and full multi-scope sort support (`HOT`, `NEW`, `TOP`) across NEARBY, AREA, CITY, and DISTRICT scopes.
- `PostService` & `SocialPostService`: Core CRUD, batch DTO conversions, batch interaction checks, Cloudinary media processing, and Neighborhood Q&A 4-layer geo-scoping with auto-coordinate resolution from `PincodeLookup` and cursor pagination.
- `PostInteractionService` & `CommentService`: Real-time post interactions with atomic counter database updates, Spring cache management (`postCounts`), automated self-healing comment count synchronization, `PaginatedResponse` `totalElements` propagation, and STOMP WebSocket broadcast updates (`/topic/feed.updates`, `/topic/post.{postId}.updates`, `/topic/community.{id}.updates`).
- `PollService`: Poll lifecycle management, flexible duration calculations (including perpetual/Always voting), option management, and batch vote recording.
- `TranslationService`: Multi-lingual translation with non-blocking virtual threads, 300ms feed timeout guard, and DB/API tier caching (`translationsApi`).
- `PinCodeLookupService`: Geocoding and geographic radius calculations with Caffeine caching (`pincode_nearby`, `pincode-data`).
- `UserTaggingService`: Mentions and user tagging with batch lookup optimization.
- `ChatSessionService`: WebSocket routing, ephemeral media lifecycle, and selective audit persistence filtering with configurable regex patterns (`chat.audit.excluded-email-regex`).
- `MatchmakingService`: Location-aware stranger chat matching.
- `AuthController` & `GoogleOAuthConfig`: High-performance OAuth2 authentication pipeline with singleton in-memory `GoogleIdTokenVerifier` certificate caching, zero redundant DB round-trips, direct UserDetails JWT generation, and atomic refresh token rotation via `RefreshTokenService.createRefreshToken(User)`.
  - `POST /api/auth/vault-blob` & `POST /api/auth/vault/setup`: Encrypts/saves client vault blob, updates `User` vault metadata, and registers requested or generated pseudonym in `ActorProfile`.
  - `POST /api/auth/reset-vault`: Re-encrypts client vault, rotates `actor_token`, and binds the new `ActorProfile` persona (with requested custom or randomized pseudonym).
  - `POST /api/auth/vault/init-reset`: Invalidates current encrypted vault and provides a fresh `seedBlindSalt` for new PIN derivation without asking the user to re-enter persona fields.
  - `GET /api/auth/generate-pseudonym`: Generates a guaranteed unique, non-colliding civic pseudonym verified across both `actor_profiles` and `users` tables.
- `SearchController` & `SearchService`: Unified multi-type search engine (POST, SOCIAL_POST, COMMUNITY, HASHTAG) supporting cursor/offset pagination, typeahead autocomplete, and tag-aware multi-entity search that surfaces related civic posts, hashtag-indexed social discussions, and matching communities.
- `WebSocketConfig`: Inbound STOMP channel security interceptor with graceful unauthenticated connection filtering, user tracking, and session IP resolution.

## Architectural Patterns & Caching

- **Zero-Knowledge Blind Shield (Decoupled Civic Architecture)**:
  - Citizens (`ROLE_USER`): Civic content (`posts`, `social_posts`, `comments`, `post_likes`, `saved_posts`, `user_tags`, `polls`, `poll_votes`) has `user_id = NULL` and author identity linked exclusively via cryptographic `actor_token` (`act_...`), strictly complying with database check constraints (`chk_post_author`, `chk_social_post_author`, `chk_comment_author`).
  - Authorities (`ROLE_DEPARTMENT`, `ROLE_ADMIN`): Government official broadcasts and department actions retain `user_id` with `actor_token = NULL`.
  - Posting, Comments & Snapshots: `CommentService`, `SocialPostService`, `PostService`, and `PollService` extract `X-Actor-Token` headers via `resolveActorToken()`, decoupling citizen content (`user_id = null`, `actor_token = token`) and snapshotting `author_username` (civic pseudonym) and `author_profile_image`.
  - Eager Fetching Safety: Repository bulk fetches (such as `SocialPostRepo.findAllByIdsWithUserAndCommunity`) use `LEFT JOIN FETCH p.user` to prevent citizen posts (`user_id = NULL`) from being filtered out.
  - "Ask Locally" (Neighborhood Wisdom / Q&A): Posts under `category = NEIGHBORHOOD_QUESTION` anchor to home or target pincodes (`qaPosterScope = "ASK_ANYWHERE"` / `"HOME"`) with latitude, longitude, and urban cluster lookups while maintaining zero-knowledge isolation.
  - Civic Post & Display Eligibility: `PostUtility.isPostEligibleForDisplay` and `SocialPost.isEligibleForDisplay` evaluate `true` when `user == null` for decoupled citizen posts with `PostStatus.ACTIVE`, allowing interactions (likes, dislikes, saves, shares, comments) to succeed without 400 validation exceptions. `Post.hasPincodeLocation()` and `Post.getPostPincode()` check `author_pincode` snapshot first, preserving geographic feed relevance.
  - Polls & Anonymous Voting: `PollService` snapshots `actor_token`, `author_username`, and `author_profile_image` on the parent `SocialPost`. `Poll.createdByActorToken` and `PollVote.actorToken` store citizen pseudonyms without linking `user_id`. `PollController.vote` flexibly accepts both JSON arrays and object payloads, allowing poll creation, voting, closing, and deleting to operate seamlessly under strict zero-knowledge isolation.
  - Ownership & Permission Resolution: `PostUtility.isPostOwner`, `SocialPostUtility.isSocialPostOwner`, and `CommentService` check `actor_token` equality for citizen posts/comments and fallback to `user.getId()` for authorities (`ROLE_DEPARTMENT`, `ROLE_ADMIN`), ensuring citizens can edit/delete their own content.
  - Department Tagging & Mentions: `UserTaggingService` preserves `tagged_by_actor_token` and `tagged_by_username` on `UserTag` records, ensuring citizen mentions of departments work without relational foreign keys.
  - Feed Query Compatibility: Feed queries in `PostRepo` and `SocialPostRepo` use `LEFT JOIN` / direct post column filters (`sp.pincode`, `p.authorPincode`) rather than `user.pincode`, preventing inner-join dropouts of decoupled citizen posts.
  - Persona & Count Resolution: `GET /api/users/me` uses `ActorProfileService.createOrCopyFromUser(token, user)` and `X-Actor-Token` / `serverActorToken` derivation to ensure citizen pseudonyms are accurately resolved and assigned. Internal `acc_...` keys are sanitized at both backend and frontend layers to prevent raw database keys from leaking. Counts (`/api/posts/count/my-posts`, `/api/social-posts/count/my-posts`, `/api/communities/count/me`) query by `actor_token` with fallback to `countByUserId`, while `IdentityBlindService.resolveActorTokenForUser` falls back to `ActorProfileRepo.findByUsername`. Frontend Profile warms up `vaultService` cache, supplies `X-Actor-Token` explicitly, and dynamically reconciles `socialCount` with `socialPosts.length` to eliminate intermittent 0 count renders on page load/reload.
  - Interaction & Activity Pipelines: `PostInteractionController`, `PostInteractionService`, `CommentService`, `CommentInteractionController`, `CommunityController`, and `CommunityService` inspect `X-Actor-Token` from the HTTP header across `/saved`, `/saved/social-posts`, `/saved/posts`, `/liked`, `/commented`, `/interactions`, `/api/communities/{id}/posts`, `/api/communities/{id}/posts/top`, `/api/communities/slug/{slug}/posts`, and `/api/communities/{id}/posts/user/{userId}`. `PostInteractionService` explicitly forwards `actorToken` to background `@Async("taskExecutor")` workers (`executeLikeSocialPostAsync`, `executeDislikeSocialPostAsync`, `executeLikePostAsync`, `executeDislikePostAsync`, `executeToggleSocialPostSaveAsync`, `executeToggleBroadcastPostSaveAsync`), preventing asynchronous context loss when `RequestContextHolder` is detached on thread pools. Citizen interactions and saved posts are recorded with `user_id = NULL` and `actor_token = "act_..."` via `SavedPostRepo.findByUserIdOrActorTokenOrderBySavedAtDesc`, while authorities (`ROLE_DEPARTMENT`, `ROLE_ADMIN`) retain relational `user_id`. `CommunityService` leverages `findLikedSocialPostIdsByActorOrUser` and `findSavedSocialPostIdsByActorOrUser` to compute `isLikedByMe` and `isSavedByMe` for decoupled citizens, evaluates `isMyPost` based on `actor_token` equality, and delegates "My Posts" filtering to `SocialPostRepo.findCommunityPostsByUserOrActorTokenCursorAndStatuses`. In the Govlyx frontend, `Communities.tsx` synchronizes `saveCount` and dispatches `onCommentCountChange` to `PostCard` to keep real-time UI counters consistent across interactions. `CommentService` enforces null-safe checks on `allowComments` and `status` to prevent 400 Bad Request rejections on legacy/uninitialized post rows, and integrates `ActorProfileService` to dynamically resolve unique civic pseudonyms. `CommentDto` and `SocialPostDto` prioritize public pseudonyms (`authorUsername`) and sanitize citizen accounts against raw `acc_...` or `act_...` keys. `validateSocialPostInteractable`, `CommentService.validateSocialPost`, and `PollService` incorporate safe fallbacks to `community.getPrivacy()` to prevent false 400/403 rejections on legacy community posts.
  - Client-Side Dual Caching & Silent Token Derivation: `vaultService.ts` synchronizes derived actor tokens across both `sessionStorage` and `localStorage` to avoid cold-start race conditions. JWT tokens and auth responses embed `serverActorToken` to enable immediate client-side HMAC derivation. Axios interceptors automatically attach `X-Actor-Token` on every request.
  - Authorities (`ROLE_DEPARTMENT`, `ROLE_ADMIN`): Relational `user_id` foreign keys are permanently preserved to guarantee statutory public traceability and official department badges.
  - Zero-Knowledge Client Vault: Server stores only client-encrypted `vault_blob` and never stores the user's secret PIN or client decryption salt.
  - Persona Synchronization: All persona settings are mirrored on `User`. Even if an author token is rotated or reset, 100% of persona attributes are cloned to the new `ActorProfile` with zero user friction.
  - Profile, Location & Email Security Lifecycle:
    - **Email Security & Blind Indexing**: `User.email` is encrypted at rest using AES-GCM (`AesGcmEmailConverter`). Email existence and uniqueness checks utilize HMAC-SHA256 blind indexing (`existsByEmailHash(identityBlindService.deriveEmailHash(email))`). Email verification tokens are generated upon registration and resent via `/api/auth/resend-verification` with blind email lookup, and transactional verification links are delivered via Brevo REST API using sanitized persona greetings ("Citizen" / pseudonyms) without exposing internal account keys. Email updates enter a pending state with single-dispatch verification tokens. When confirmed via `/verify-email-update`, both `email` and `email_hash` are atomically updated alongside programmatic cache eviction, ensuring authentication continuity.
    - **Pincode & GPS Center Sync**: Manual pincode updates via `PUT /api/users/update-pincode` or `PUT /api/users/profile` automatically synchronize the user's `homeLatitude` and `homeLongitude` to the exact centroid of that Indian postal code, automatically repositioning the NEARBY 5km feed scope.
    - **Live Device GPS Geocoding**: `PUT /api/users/location` updates device coordinates and auto-syncs reverse-geocoded pincodes while preventing data drift between `users` and `actor_profiles`.
    - **Frontend Display**: `Profile.tsx` displays verified/unverified badges, pending verification tags, active coordinate status, and inline Enter/Save handlers without value suppression.
- **Multi-Level Caffeine Caching**: Managed by `CacheConfig` with `SimpleCacheManager` and `TransactionAwareCacheManagerProxy`:
  - `hlig_feed`: High-throughput feed candidate caching (TTL: 2m).
  - `pincode_nearby`: Haversine distance and adjacent pincodes lookup (TTL: 30m).
  - `pincode-data`: Static geographic reference data (TTL: 24h).
  - `translationsApi`: External API translation cache (TTL: 2h).
  - `postCounts`: Active interaction counts map for single posts and social posts with programmatic eviction on interactions/comments.
  - `authUserDetails`, `user-profiles` (`CACHE_USER_PROFILE`): Profile and session caches evicted on user/theme updates so `/api/users/me` immediately delivers updated preferences.
  - `community-list`, `communities`.
- **Google Public Keys In-Memory Caching**: `GoogleOAuthConfig` configures a thread-safe singleton `GoogleIdTokenVerifier` preventing repetitive outbound HTTPS certificate downloads.
- **Real-Time STOMP Event Broadcasting**: Endpoints emit full `STATS_UPDATE` payloads over WebSocket broker for live multi-client synchronization of likes, dislikes, shares, comments, saves, and views.
- **Non-Blocking External Integrations**: Virtual threads for I/O with strict bounded timeouts (e.g. 300ms) to ensure feed endpoints never block.
- **Batch Querying & Eager Fetching**: Batch loads for author roles, member statuses, tags, and `@EntityGraph` eager loading on `PostLikeRepo`, `SavedPostRepo`, and `CommentRepo` activity lookups to eliminate N+1 queries.
- **User Activity Lookups**: Dedicated paginated activity lookup endpoints in `PostInteractionController` (`/api/interactions/{liked, commented, saved}`) with soft-delete exclusion, chronological creation-date ordering, and accurate `LIKE` vs `DISLIKE` reaction state fidelity.
- **Scheduled Tasks**: Background monitoring for SLAs, community health scores, view count buffering (`ViewCountFlusher`), and metrics. All 15+ `@Scheduled` tasks run on a dedicated 5-thread `ThreadPoolTaskScheduler` (bean: `taskScheduler` in `AsyncConfig`), preventing any single slow task from blocking unrelated tasks (scalability audit M-3). Community deletion tasks slowed to 5-min / 10-min cron cadence (was 60 s) — grace periods are hours-scale, not seconds-scale (scalability audit C-2).
- **Database & Pre-JPA Data Hygiene**:
  - `DatabasePreMigrationCleanup`: Configured with `EntityManagerFactoryDependsOnPostProcessor` to guarantee execution _before_ Hibernate schema validation. Purges orphaned records across interaction and civic tables (e.g., `post_likes`, `post_views`, `comments`, `saved_posts` referencing non-existent posts) so that foreign key constraints can be applied cleanly without violating relational integrity.
  - `ddl-auto=validate`: Flyway exclusively owns all schema migrations (V1–V5). Hibernate only validates that entity models match the live schema; it no longer performs its own schema diffing on startup (scalability audit H-1, eliminates 2–5 s boot overhead and schema drift risk).
  - `V5__decouple_citizen_usernames.sql` & `DatabaseMigrationRunner`: Decouples all `ROLE_USER` citizen accounts to opaque `acc_<id>_<hash>` keys, permanently severing cross-table correlation between `users` and `actor_profiles`/civic content. Zero citizen overlap remains between `users.username` and `actor_profiles.username`.
  - PostgreSQL with Hibernate spatial/indexing optimizations and database counter reconciliation scripts.
- **Security & Authentication Provider Architecture**:
  - Unified `UserDetailsService`: `CustomUserDetailsService` designated as `@Primary` and sole `UserDetailsService` bean, fully encapsulating zero-knowledge blind email hashing, and user credential validation.
  - `SecurityConfig` instantiates `ProviderManager` wrapping `DaoAuthenticationProvider`, eliminating redundant global authentication provider collisions and deprecation warnings.
